Email Enrichment Project — 2,379 Professional Records Cleaned & Verified

03

The Challenge

A fintech client had 2,379 professional contacts in their database with no verified email addresses — making newsletter and cold email launch impossible. Records had inconsistent formatting, missing names, and no LinkedIn verification. The goal: clean, verify, and segment the full list ahead of a mass email launch.

What I Built

  • Built a Safe Manual Bridge workflow using Apollo People Search (never Chrome Extension) to protect account health
  • Designed a free-tool waterfall: Skrapp → Prospeo → Tomba → BounceGuard for records Apollo couldn’t match
  • Validated emails via Mailmeteor for bounce risk before adding to send list
  • Organized clean Google Sheets with segmented tabs: Verified LinkedIn, Verified Email, No Name Records, APOLLO, Mailmeteor
  • Flagged 298 no-name records as unworkable and isolated for separate handling
  • Delivered final enriched list ready for newsletter launch — segmented, clean, and bounce-validated
